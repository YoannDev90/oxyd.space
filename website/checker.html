Naming rules

  • 1–4 labels separated by dots (e.g. api.myname)
  • Lowercase a–z, digits 0–9, hyphens (no leading/trailing hyphen)
  • 63 characters per label
  • Some names are reserved (www, root, ns, api…)

Supported record types

CNAME websites & hosting A servers & IPv4 AAAA servers & IPv6 TXT verifications
